Nutrition & Education

Fuel that matches your goal.

We're not registered dietitians, but nutrition guidance is built into every program. Here's a quick look at how we group foods by training goal. Tap a card for details.

Carbohydrates +

Fuel for training & recovery.

Peak 1 (leanest): Alfalfa sprout, artichoke, asparagus, bell pepper, broccoli, brussels sprouts, cabbage, cauliflower, celery, collard greens, cucumber, eggplant, garlic, romaine lettuce, mushroom, parsley, peas, pickle, radish, salsa, sauerkraut, squash, and mixed steamed veggies.

Light in calories but nutrient-dense — vital when the goal is fat loss, and lowest on the glycemic scale.

Proteins +

Repair & build lean muscle.

Fish & seafood: Alaskan crab, blue crab, clams, cod, grouper, flounder, haddock, halibut, lobster, mussels, and more.

Meat & poultry: Skinless chicken breast, egg whites, turkey breast, lean burger, filet mignon, lean ham & pork.

Dairy: Cottage cheese, fat-free cheese, low-fat yogurt, 1% milk.

Vegetarian: Meatless burger, soy burger/sausage, tofu.

Healthy Fats +

Omega-3s in the right amounts.

Nuts: Almonds, brazil nuts, walnuts, pistachios, pecans, macadamia.

Seeds: Pumpkin, sunflower.

Produce: Avocado, guacamole, olives.

Oils: Coconut & olive (good for cooking); flax & krill (use caution when cooking).

Healthy fats are calorie-dense in small volume — portion matters even when the source is "good."

Peak Fruits +

The most powerful picks.

Avocado: raw fat most people are deficient in, more potassium than a banana.

Berries: antioxidant-rich, high fiber, low sugar.

Guava: vitamin C, lycopene, fiber, may help lower LDL cholesterol.

Kiwi & Mango: vitamins C & E, potassium, folate.

Papaya & Pineapple: digestive enzymes, anti-inflammatory support, immune support.

The Science Behind It

Paul's programming draws on myofascial "functional line" training — the idea that muscles work in connected chains across the body, not in isolation. It's part of why sessions emphasize posture, alignment, and full-body coordination, not just reps.
